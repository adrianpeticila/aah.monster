## voice is a document, not a talent

People treat their writing voice as something mystical. It is not. It is a set of measurable habits: how long your sentences run before you cut them, which words you never use, how you open, whether you land the ending or leave it hanging. All of it can be written down.

Once it is written down, it stops being yours alone. You can hand it to a ghostwriter, an assistant, a head of comms, or a machine, and get back something that sounds like you instead of like everyone.

**Nobody can write in your voice if nobody has ever described it.** Most executives have paid for years of content without anyone doing that once.
