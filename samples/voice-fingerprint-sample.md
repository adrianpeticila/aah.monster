# Voice Fingerprint — sample deliverable · aah, monster!

> **Sample** of the written voice record for €249 (48h). The real document is built from 15–20 samples of your own writing; excerpts below are illustrative.

**Product:** Voice Fingerprint · https://aah.monster/voice-fingerprint/

## 01 — rhythm signature

| trait | your pattern | never "fix" it |
|---|---|---|
| sentence length | short opener, long closer | never equalize |
| punctuation | dashes over semicolons | no semicolon polish |
| paragraphs | 1–3 lines, hard breaks | no 8-line blocks |

## 02 — what the full document contains

- sentence rhythm and where you cut
- punctuation you avoid (and the ones you overuse)
- how you open a post / how you land it
- **never-list**: words and moves that are not yours ("thrilled to announce", emoji openers…)
- gap analysis: where your published writing already drifts from your spoken voice
- three rewrites of your own posts — in your voice, not mine
- one-page brief you forward to anyone who writes for you

## 03 — sample excerpt

> never-list entry: "excited to share" — you never say it out loud; it only shows up when a draft was written *for* you, not *by* you.

## 04 — who this is for

founders and executives whose ghostwriter, team, or AI tool needs a source of truth — not another style guide nobody reads.
