## 01 — verdict (page 1)

one line: what a stranger concludes about you in 10 seconds, and whether that conclusion wins you the deals you want.

| signal | verdict | severity |
|---|---|---|
| headline | job title, not point of view | critical |
| about section | cv in paragraph form | critical |
| google footprint | name resolves to nothing | leak |
| content cadence | last post > 90 days | leak |
| audience specificity | posts for "everyone" | critical |

grade scale: A / C (weak differentiation) / F (terminal) — the same scale as the public MCP audit tool at https://aah.monster/mcp/

## 02 — sections in the full document

1. profile teardown — headline, about, featured, activity: line-by-line notes
2. google presence — what a buyer finds when they search your name
3. messaging gaps — claims with no evidence, evidence with no claims
4. positioning failures — the category you sound like vs. the category you win in
5. content strategy — topic, cadence, point of view; what to stop posting
6. fix order — the exact sequence, cheapest first

## 03 — sample excerpt

> headline: "VP Engineering | Helping teams ship"
> verdict: describes the job, not the belief. nobody shares a job description.
> rewrite direction: one sentence with a stance a disagreeable reader could argue with.
