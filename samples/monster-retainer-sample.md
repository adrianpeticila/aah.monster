# The Monster on Retainer — sample deliverable · aah, monster!

> **Sample** of the €2,000/month ongoing engagement. Monthly written deliverables, no calls.

**Product:** The Monster on Retainer · https://aah.monster/monster-retainer/

## monthly scope

- **16 posts per month** in your voice (4/week), dated to a shared calendar
- **monthly brand strategy memo** — what the month's content is doing to your positioning, one page
- **positioning refinement** — guardrails updated as the market (or your company) shifts
- **linkedin optimization** — headline/about/experience tuning when signals change

## what a monthly memo looks like

| section | content |
|---|---|
| signal | what performed, what got ignored (numbers, no vibes) |
| drift check | any post that pulled you off-category (and the fix) |
| next month | one theme, the argument it makes, the posts that carry it |

## rules of engagement

- async only: written input in, written deliverables out
- one round of notes per batch, in writing
- cancel anytime; the calendar and voice record stay yours
