## who this is for

a C-suite executive who has become the bottleneck: every important answer routes through one person, and the company stalls when that person is in back-to-back calls.

## what the document contains

### phase 01 — stabilize the immediate leak
- decision inventory: the 15–25 decisions currently landing on your desk weekly
- triage rules: what gets answered by whom, in writing, without you
- the "never re-decide" list: decisions that become policy after first ruling

### phase 02 — systematize the repeatable decisions
- decision tree per recurring decision type (trigger → rule → template answer)
- escalation ladder with hard limits (amount, risk, novelty)
- written SLAs: what "fast" means per decision class

### phase 03 — scale it without you
- configuration files: templates, prompts, and answer patterns ready to hand off
- 30-day operating checklist (week-by-week rollout)
- failure modes: where written systems rot, and the review cadence that prevents it

## sample excerpt — one decision rule

> **Trigger:** pricing exception request >10%.
> **Rule:** approved by deal desk if margin ≥60% and referenceable logo; otherwise escalated, never silently discounted.
> **Template:** "we don't discount the number, we change the scope — here are the three scope levers…"
