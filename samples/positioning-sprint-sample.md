## what lands at the end of day 5

| asset | what it fixes |
|---|---|
| positioning document | one sentence a stranger repeats correctly |
| linkedin headline + about rewrite | stranger test passed in under 10 seconds |
| messaging framework | 3 pillars × proof points — what you say, what you never say |
| brand voice guide | tone bounds for you, your team, and any writer/AI tool |

## sample excerpt — messaging framework

| pillar | proof | never-say |
|---|---|---|
| written thinking over performed thinking | 1 doc, 0 calls, receipts attached | "synergy", "10x your presence" |
| specificity as a filter | named buyer, named problem | "helping brands grow" |

## schedule

- day 1–2: input audit (existing content, profile, market language)
- day 3: positioning draft, one round of written notes from you
- day 4: headline/about rewrite + messaging framework
- day 5: voice guide + final bundle (one folder, one document)
