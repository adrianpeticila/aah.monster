## the 10 questions (yes/no)

01. can a stranger tell who you help and why in under 10 seconds from your linkedin headline?
02. does your linkedin about section have a point of view, or is it a cv in paragraph form?
03. have you posted on linkedin in the last 14 days?
04. does your content attract the audience you want, or the audience you already have?
05. do you have one topic you're known for, or do you post about everything?
06. when someone googles your name, do they find something useful, or nothing?
07. is your brand consistent across linkedin, your website, and how you introduce yourself in person?
08. does your content ask people to think differently, or does it just confirm what they already believe?
09. are you the most credible person in your category online, or is someone with half your experience outranking you?
10. if you disappeared from linkedin for 3 months, would anyone notice?

## scoring (in the full PDF)
