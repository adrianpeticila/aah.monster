# The Monster Job — sample deliverable · aah, monster!

> **Sample** of the €2,500 full brand build (4 weeks, fully async, written deliverables only).

**Product:** The Monster Job · https://aah.monster/the-monster-job/

## what lands (one folder, week 4)

- **positioning document** — category, wedge, the sentence people repeat
- **voice record** — how you write, with the never-list (from the Voice Fingerprint method)
- **linkedin rewrite** — headline, about, featured, banner copy
- **30 posts ready to publish** — in your voice, dated to a 30-day calendar
- **messaging framework** — pillars, proof, never-say
- **visual identity brief** — type, color, contrast rules a designer can execute cold

## week-by-week

| week | focus |
|---|---|
| 1 | inputs: interviews by written questionnaire + content dump audit |
| 2 | positioning + voice locked (you approve in writing, one round) |
| 3 | linkedin rewrite + messaging framework |
| 4 | 30 posts written, calendar set, identity brief delivered |
