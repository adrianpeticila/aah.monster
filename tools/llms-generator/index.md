# LLMs.txt Generator · aah, monster!

> A free, client-side utility that helps you format your personal brand for AI agents. Structure your site for AI crawlers.

**URL:** https://aah.monster/tools/llms-generator/ · **Author:** Adrian M. Peticila

## What it does

Generates an [llms.txt](https://llmstxt.org/) file for your personal brand. Everything runs in the browser — no account, no upload, no server call.

## Inputs

| Field | Required | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Brand Name | yes | Name heading the llms.txt summary block |
| What do you do? | yes | One-line description AI crawlers quote as your positioning |
| Website URL | no | Canonical link appended to the output |
| AI Instructions | no | Custom directives section (how agents should represent you) |

## Output

- Live Markdown preview of the llms.txt document
- **Copy** to clipboard
- **Download** as `llms.txt` — drop it in your site root and AI crawlers can read your positioning directly

## Why it exists

Most personal brands are invisible to AI agents because their positioning lives in images, carousels, and LinkedIn posts no crawler can parse. The llms.txt file is the deterministic, agent-readable version of your brand — same data, zero fluff.
