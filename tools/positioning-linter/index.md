# B2B Positioning Linter · aah, monster!

> An interactive diagnostic tool for founders and executives. Paste your LinkedIn headline, bio, or landing page hero hook. Get an instant, cynical analysis of why buyers view you as a commodity.

## What it tests
1. **Slop & Buzzword Density:** Flags generic filler like "seamlessly", "empower", "revolutionize", "leverage", "next-gen".
2. **Resume Title Dumping:** Detects when founders cram multiple disconnected credentials into one line.
3. **Apologetic 'Helping' Syndrome:** Identifies weak, passive syntax like "we help startups grow" instead of owning an operational problem.
4. **Commercial Stakes:** Checks for quantified impact (dollar amounts, hours saved, percentages).
