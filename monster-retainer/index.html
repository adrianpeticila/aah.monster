04 — faq04
ANSWERS.

what exactly do i get each month?

16 posts in your voice (ready to schedule), one async brand strategy review, positioning refinement pass, linkedin profile optimization as needed, content calendar for the next month, and quarterly a deeper positioning refresh. all delivered in writing, no calls.

how does "in your voice" work?

the first month includes a deeper onboarding: past posts, voice references, examples of writing you admire. every post goes through that filter. if a post doesn't sound like you, you push back and it gets rewritten.

how many retainer clients do you take?

limited capacity. application required. the work is intensive by design: current slots vary; ask if you want one.

can i cancel any month?

yes. cancel from your stripe dashboard at any time, no questions asked, no exit call. the month you've paid for ships. the next month doesn't.

what if i only need 8 posts/month?

then the brand positioning sprint (€499) gets you set up, and you write the cadence yourself. the retainer is for the founder who has decided the brand is worth 16 posts/month of dedicated senior focus.

is this a subscription or a project?

subscription. €2,000/month billed monthly via stripe. you can pause, cancel, or upgrade to The Monster Job at any point.

what's the difference vs The Monster Job?

The Monster Job is the foundation: eight deliverables, four weeks, one-time €2,500. The Retainer is what comes after: ongoing strategy and content. many clients do The Job first, then move to retainer once the foundation is built.
