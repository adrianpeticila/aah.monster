# FAQ | aah, monster! | aah.monster

> Canonical: https://aah.monster/faq/ | Last updated: 2026-10-14

## 1. what is llm citation optimization?
making your brand the answer AI assistants cite: Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT. Entity clarity, proof pages, quote-ready copy.

## 2. will I show up in Perplexity and Google AI Overviews?
that is the target: clean entity, one claim per page, verifiable proof links, llms.txt machines can parse. No hacks.

## 3. how fast is the audit?
48 hours, async. You send URLs and answers; you get a written audit document. No kickoff call.

## 4. do you give theory or code diffs?
both, but diffs win: exact headline rewrites, entity blocks, schema patches you can paste.

## 5. who is this for?
founders and c-suite, 1-15 people, B2B. Individuals, not committees. Contact hello@aah.monster.

## 6. do you do calls?
no. one document, no calls. If you need weekly standups, we are not a fit.

## 7. what do you need from me?
homepage URL, LinkedIn URL, offer page, last 10 lost deals in one line each.

## 8. how is this different from SEO?
SEO chases rankings; LLMO chases citations. Pages both crawlers and models can quote.

## 9. do you guarantee citations?
no. Shippable fixes that raise citation probability: entity, proof, structure.

## 10. how do we start and stop?
start with the audit (48h). Monthly after that, no long lock-in.
