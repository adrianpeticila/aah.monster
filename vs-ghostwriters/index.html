Not a single enterprise buyer, corporate board member, or venture capitalist is in that crowd. Worse: peer executives who see that content quietly lose respect for your judgment.

Freelancers cannot write without interviewing you every single week. You are paying them thousands of dollars, yet you still spend an hour on Zoom explaining your business, followed by another 30 minutes reviewing drafts that missed the point entirely.
